## 2. Goal → recommended protocol

- **First choice:** Retatrutide
- **Second / support:** 5-Amino-1MQ
- **Other options:** AOD-9604; Semaglutide; Tirzepatide
- Use Retatrutide as the primary weight-loss candidate. Add 5-Amino-1MQ only when body-composition or metabolic support is appropriate. Use AOD-9604, Semaglutide, or Tirzepatide only when the client’s stated goals, prior experience, schedule, budget, and preference support an alternative. Cagrilintide is excluded until an Active dosing standard exists. Pen presentation is available only when explicitly requested and does not change compound selection priority.

- **First choice:** Tesamorelin
- **Second / support:** 5-Amino-1MQ
- **Other options:** CJC-1295 without DAC + Ipamorelin; AOD-9604; MOTS-C

## 4. How to talk about each compound

Use the *client-facing wording* on calls. It is EVOLV's own approved language, so it's what the closer can say with certainty. The *don't claim* line is EVOLV's boundary for that compound.

### Tesamorelin
- **What it is:** A synthetic growth-hormone-releasing hormone analogue studied in humans. Approved tesamorelin drug products have evidence for reducing excess visceral abdominal fat in adults with HIV-associated lipodystrophy. That evidence does not establish general weight-loss use or the quality, safety, or equivalence of a research vial or supplier-prepared pen.
- **Say:** May be described as supporting GH-axis signalling and body-composition goals, with the approved indication and population kept explicit.
- **Evidence level:** Established human clinical evidence for a specific approved-drug use
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim broad fat loss, muscle gain, anti-aging, or equivalence to an approved tesamorelin product.

### CJC-1295 without DAC + Ipamorelin
- **What it is:** CJC-1295 without DAC plus Ipamorelin is governed in two exact EVOLV presentations: a 10 mg vial containing 5 mg of each component and, when explicitly requested, a 20 mg supplier-prepared pen containing 10 mg of each component. The protocol rationale is based on the individual components. Neither exact finished blend presentation has been established as a clinically validated combination.
- **Say:** May be described as a GH-axis support blend selected for body-composition or recovery-oriented educational goals.
- **Evidence level:** Limited human component evidence; exact blend not established
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven muscle gain, fat loss, healing, anti-aging, or blend-specific safety/effectiveness.

### 5-Amino-1MQ
- **What it is:** A small-molecule NNMT inhibitor studied mainly in cell and animal models, including diet-induced obesity models. Established human efficacy and a validated injectable regimen are not available.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational metabolic or body-composition support concept based mainly on preclinical research.
- **Evidence level:** Preclinical evidence
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven human fat loss, metabolic improvement, or safety.

### Retatrutide
- **What it is:** An investigational triple agonist targeting GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors. Phase 2 human research reported substantial average weight reduction, but retatrutide remains distinct from an approved commercial drug product and the research-vial or supplier-prepared pen presentation is not established by the trial.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational weight-management compound with human trial evidence.
- **Evidence level:** Human investigational evidence
- **Don't claim:** Do not call it approved, guaranteed, or equivalent to the trial product.

### BPC-157
- **What it is:** A synthetic peptide discussed for tissue-repair and gastrointestinal research. Most supportive evidence is preclinical, and a validated approved human formulation, indication, and dosing regimen are not established.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational recovery or tissue-support concept with predominantly preclinical evidence.
- **Evidence level:** Predominantly preclinical and limited human evidence
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven injury healing, tendon repair, gut healing, or established human safety.

### GHK-Cu
- **What it is:** An endogenous copper-binding peptide studied for skin, wound, and tissue-remodelling biology. Much of the human-facing evidence relates to topical or dermatologic contexts rather than subcutaneous research-vial use.
- **Say:** May be described as a skin and tissue-support peptide with route-specific evidence limitations.
- **Evidence level:** Human topical/dermatologic context plus preclinical evidence; subcutaneous use not established
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim established subcutaneous anti-aging, hair-growth, wound-healing, or systemic effectiveness.

### KLOW
- **What it is:** KLOW combines GHK-Cu 50 mg, BPC-157 10 mg, TB-500 10 mg, and KPV 10 mg in an 80 mg total blend. The protocol rationale is based on the individual components. The exact finished blend has not been established as a clinically validated combination.
- **Say:** May be described as a multi-component skin, recovery, and inflammation-support concept based on the individual components.
- **Evidence level:** Component extrapolation; exact blend not clinically established
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim blend-specific efficacy, synergy, safety, or superior outcomes.

### Wolverine Stack: BPC-157 + TB-500
- **What it is:** Wolverine combines BPC-157 and TB-500. Wolverine 10 mg contains 5 mg of each component, and Wolverine 20 mg contains 10 mg of each component. The protocol rationale is based on the individual components. The exact finished blend has not been established as a clinically validated combination.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational recovery-oriented blend based on component research.
- **Evidence level:** Component extrapolation; exact blend not clinically established
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven injury healing, faster recovery, blend synergy, or established human safety.

### GLOW
- **What it is:** GLOW combines GHK-Cu 50 mg, BPC-157 10 mg, and TB-500 10 mg in a 70 mg total blend. The protocol rationale is based on the individual components. The exact finished blend has not been established as a clinically validated combination.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational skin and recovery-support blend based on component research.
- **Evidence level:** Component extrapolation; exact blend not clinically established
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven skin rejuvenation, healing, anti-aging, synergy, or blend-specific safety.

### AOD-9604
- **What it is:** A modified growth-hormone fragment investigated for metabolic and obesity-related purposes. Available research includes preclinical work and human safety exposure, but reliable human weight-loss efficacy has not been established.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational metabolic or body-composition compound with limited and mixed evidence.
- **Evidence level:** Human investigational safety data and preclinical metabolic evidence; efficacy not established
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven fat burning, targeted fat loss, or established weight-loss efficacy.

### DSIP
- **What it is:** Delta sleep-inducing peptide has been examined in small older human sleep studies, with inconsistent findings and no established modern therapeutic role.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational sleep-support peptide with limited and mixed human evidence.
- **Evidence level:** Small, older human studies with mixed evidence
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim reliable sleep improvement, insomnia treatment, or established safety.

### Epitalon (Epithalon)
- **What it is:** A synthetic tetrapeptide studied in cellular and preclinical aging-related research. Credible controlled human evidence for longevity, telomere extension, or anti-aging outcomes is not established.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational longevity-research peptide with predominantly preclinical evidence.
- **Evidence level:** Predominantly cell and animal evidence
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim life extension, telomere restoration, age reversal, or proven human anti-aging effects.

### Glutathione
- **What it is:** An endogenous antioxidant with human research across oral and intravenous routes. Route, formulation, and outcome differences matter; the cited evidence does not establish the governed subcutaneous research-vial protocol.
- **Say:** May be described as an antioxidant-support compound with human evidence that is route dependent.
- **Evidence level:** Human evidence varies by route; governed subcutaneous use not established by cited studies
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim detoxification, disease treatment, guaranteed skin effects, or established subcutaneous safety/effectiveness.

### IGF-1 LR3
- **What it is:** A long-acting IGF-1 analogue used in research. Published evidence located for this reference is largely animal or anti-doping analytical work rather than controlled therapeutic human trials.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational growth-signalling compound used in research contexts.
- **Evidence level:** Research analogue with primarily animal and analytical evidence
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven muscle gain, recovery, performance enhancement, or established human therapeutic safety.

### KPV
- **What it is:** A tripeptide fragment of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone studied in cell and animal inflammation models. Controlled human injectable evidence is not established.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational inflammation- or gut-support concept based mainly on preclinical research.
- **Evidence level:** Preclinical evidence
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim treatment of inflammatory or gastrointestinal disease, proven symptom relief, or established human safety.

### MOTS-C
- **What it is:** A mitochondrial-derived peptide linked to metabolic and exercise biology. Human studies support its endogenous physiological relevance, while evidence for exogenous injected MOTS-C protocols remains largely preclinical or early-stage.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational mitochondrial and metabolic-support peptide.
- **Evidence level:** Endogenous human biology plus predominantly preclinical exogenous-use evidence
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven fat loss, endurance enhancement, insulin-sensitivity treatment, or established injected human safety.

### NAD+
- **What it is:** Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide is a central cellular coenzyme rather than a peptide. Small human studies and reviews have examined intravenous NAD-related administration and tolerability, but the governed subcutaneous research-vial protocol is not established by that evidence.
- **Say:** May be described as a cellular-energy or redox-support concept with limited route-specific human evidence.
- **Evidence level:** Limited human infusion research; subcutaneous use not established by cited studies
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim anti-aging, addiction treatment, cognitive restoration, energy improvement, or established subcutaneous efficacy.

### Semaglutide
- **What it is:** A GLP-1 receptor agonist used in approved prescription drug products for defined diabetes and weight-management indications. Evidence for those regulated products does not establish the identity, quality, safety, or equivalence of a research vial or supplier-prepared pen.
- **Say:** May be described as a GLP-1 compound with extensive human evidence for approved products and weight-management applications.
- **Evidence level:** Established human clinical evidence for approved drug products
- **Don't claim:** Do not state that a research vial or supplier-prepared pen is approved, equivalent to a branded drug, guaranteed, or medically suitable for this client.

### TB-500
- **What it is:** TB-500 is the official EVOLV product and active-component name. It is discussed in investigational recovery and tissue-support contexts. The current source package does not treat research on differently identified compounds as proof of TB-500 identity, safety, effectiveness, dose, or outcomes.
- **Say:** May be described as an investigational recovery or tissue-support compound with limited product-specific human evidence.
- **Evidence level:** Product-specific human evidence is limited; evidence for differently identified compounds is not treated as proof of TB-500 outcomes
- **Don't claim:** Do not claim proven systemic healing, tendon or ligament repair, guaranteed recovery, or that research on a differently identified compound proves TB-500 outcomes.

### Tirzepatide
- **What it is:** A dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist used in approved prescription drug products for defined indications. Evidence for regulated tirzepatide products does not establish the identity, quality, safety, or equivalence of a research vial or supplier-prepared pen.
- **Say:** May be described as a dual incretin compound with extensive human evidence for approved products and weight-management applications.
- **Evidence level:** Established human clinical evidence for approved drug products
