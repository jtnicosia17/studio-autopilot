### 1. Purpose and Authority

This SOP controls creation of the current customer-facing Align Biosciences Personalized Investment Summary. The exact current Align Biosciences Personalized Investment Summary PDF Template v1.3 is the visual and wording authority for the customer document. The previous EVOLV Personalized Plan & Investment Summary master and previous EVOLV summary PDF template are retired and must not be used.
The internal 12-week protocol and mandatory Builder calculation are still completed first. This SOP does not create dosing authority, product eligibility, medical suitability, or evidence claims.


### 2. Responsibility Boundary

Align Bio Science provides pricing, discounts, investment options, payment methods, purchasing, shipping, and order processing.
EVOLV provides education, consultation, protocol, dosing, and schedule support only.
Do not present EVOLV as the seller, fulfiller, manufacturer, pricing authority, payment processor, shipping service, or order-processing service.
Commercial information in the Investment Summary does not establish medical suitability, safety, authorization, or expected benefit.


### 3. Trigger and Output

Default after consultation: CREATE INVESTMENT SUMMARY. Complete the internal protocol, mandatory Builder calculation, full 12-week product requirement, and Investment Waves calculation before creating the customer PDF.
Populate the current controlled Align PDF template directly. Do not create a customer-specific Investment Summary DOCX.
Return the final customer PDF only. Do not automatically generate a matching email or SMS.
An explicit authorized requester command may bypass the normal summary-first sequence and release a phased or 12-week protocol after Builder completion and internal QA.


### 4. Page 1 Template Control

Use the Align Biosciences branding, title, customer / summary date / primary goals / optional reference fields, and Selected Products cards exactly as the current PDF template establishes.
The Investment Summary introduction is commercial/planning only. Do not add protocol dose, draw, click, reconstitution, timing, frequency, titration, or administration instructions.
Preserve the What Happens Next responsibility split: product purchase and commercial questions go to Align; education, consultation, protocol, dosing, or schedule questions go to EVOLV.
Preserve the batch-specific third-party COA statement and the current Align pricing explanation.


### 5. Page 2 Purchase Roadmap

Show three actual 4-week purchase windows: Purchase 1, Purchase 2, and Purchase 3. Product quantities may differ by purchase because products may begin, continue, end, or carry forward.
For each purchase card, show exact product/strength, quantity, BAC/carry-forward status when applicable, the current 10% Align discount, Credit Card Total, and Interac Total.
Show the complete calculated 12-week product requirement in the 12-Week Best Value table, with regular product value, Align bulk savings, subtotal after bulk pricing, 15% 12-week discount, optional Interac 5% savings, shipping, Credit Card Total, and Interac Total.
Show estimated savings versus the actual three separate 4-week purchases. Do not multiply Purchase 1 by three and do not divide the 12-week requirement into equal thirds.


### 6. Commercial Calculation Rules

Exact matching peptide SKU: single at regular price; matching 3-pack saves 6%; matching 5-pack saves 10%. Different products, strengths, or formats never combine for a bulk tier.
BAC water is added at regular price and does not receive Align bulk pricing.
Each separate 4-week purchase uses the current Align 10% discount after eligible bulk pricing.
The complete 12-Week Best Value purchase uses the current Align 15% discount after eligible bulk pricing.
Optional Interac e-Transfer adds another 5% savings from the same post-bulk subtotal. Do not compound the 5% on an already-discounted amount.
Shipping is free when the final discounted merchandise subtotal is $400 or more and $20 below $400. No HST.
Customer-facing output must not display an EVOLV-branded commercial code.


### 7. Information That Must Be Withheld

Dose per administration or delivered dose.
U-100 syringe units, pen clicks, split-dial instructions, or syringe/needle selection.
BAC-water reconstitution volume/concentration or mixing instructions.
Exact administration days, time of day, frequency, titration, holds, escalations, or phase-by-phase dose schedule.
Injection, handling, storage, or pen-use instructions beyond the non-actionable product format shown by the template.


### 8. Protocol Discussion Callout

The current template includes a Questions About Education or Your Protocol callout. The BOOK PROTOCOL DISCUSSION WITH EVOLV control may use the current Protocol & Product Review route: https://book.evolvtoday.ca/widget/bookings/15-minute-protocol-product-review-team . EVOLV may discuss education, consultation, protocol, dosing, or schedule questions only. EVOLV must not use this callout to advise on pricing, discounts, investment options, payment, purchasing, shipping, or order processing.


### 9. Soft Protocol Release

Normal business flow is Investment Summary first, then protocol after the applicable Align purchase is confirmed. This is a soft workflow gate, not a technical generation key. Do not require, request, or validate an order number before protocol generation. An explicit authorized requester release command remains sufficient after Builder completion and internal QA.


### 10. Filename and Direct-to-Final Rule

Final filename: [Client First Name]_[Client Last Name if provided]_Investment_Summary_[Month]_[Day]_[Year].pdf. Use the Summary Date. Do not infer a surname. Do not add _DRAFT or _FINAL. Generate directly from the controlled Align PDF template after the Builder calculation and QA are complete.


### 11. Final Quality Gate

Current Align Biosciences PDF Template v1.3 used; old EVOLV summary templates absent.
Customer identity, summary date, goals, product strengths/formats, quantities, carry-forward status, bulk savings, discount percentages, payment totals, shipping, and savings comparison verified.
No actionable dosing or administration data appears anywhere in the Investment Summary.
Commercial responsibility wording keeps Align and EVOLV roles separate.
No EVOLV-branded commercial code appears in customer-facing output.
Protocol discussion callout, if linked, uses the current Protocol & Product Review route and does not invite commercial advice from EVOLV.
Final PDF visually inspected. No clipping, overlap, broken glyphs, stale template text, or extra pages.
No automatic matching email or SMS generated.

### Arithmetic

- `MONTHLY_PHASE`: regular unit price × explicit quantities. No bulk optimization
or pack discounts at any quantity. Credit Card10%; Interac10% +5 percentage
points, for15% total. Both savings use the regular merchandise subtotal.
- `TWELVE_WEEK_BEST_VALUE`: exact-SKU pack optimization first, then Credit Card15%
or Interac15% +5 percentage points from the same post-bulk subtotal. There is
no20% base offer, no compounded5% and no third promotional discount.
- Matching3-packs save6%; matching5-packs save10%. Different strengths/formats/
products never pool. The unchanged historical pack arithmetic is reused only
on the Best Value path. Monthly never invokes the optimizer.
- Shipping uses discounted merchandise before shipping: CAD400 or more is free,
otherwise CAD20; zero merchandise quantities have no shipping charge. This
stage is expressly supported by S06 Commercial Rules B10:B12 and S00 PRICING.
No HST is added. All amounts retain exact Decimal values; no invented cents
rounding occurs. Settlement rounding remains a production decision.
