Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains what YouTube Autopilot (the "App") does with information it gets from Google and YouTube. The App is operated by JT Nicosia ("we") and is used only to manage YouTube channels that we own: The Little Ages, Hazel and Bo, and Retirement Rulebook.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App asks for these permissions, and uses each one only as described:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's videos and Shorts.
  • Manage YouTube videos and captions (youtube.force-ssl): to set thumbnails, titles, descriptions, captions and publish times on the owner's own videos.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel it is signed in to and to list the owner's own uploads.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): to read views, watch time, subscribers and, once a channel is monetized, its estimated revenue, so the owner can see which videos perform well.

The App receives an OAuth access token and refresh token from Google. It does not see or store the owner's Google password.

Information about viewers

The App does not collect, store or process personal information about people who watch the channels. The analytics it reads are aggregated totals per video and per channel provided by YouTube Analytics. The App does not read, store or reply to comments. The Hazel and Bo channel is directed to children and is marked "made for kids" on YouTube; the App collects no information from or about children.

How information is used and stored

  • All data stays on the owner's personal computer. Tokens are stored in a local file, and analytics reports are saved as local files for the owner's review.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer data obtained through YouTube API Services to anyone. We do not use it for advertising.
  • Video scripts and frames are prepared on the owner's computer with the help of AI tools. No data obtained from YouTube API Services is sent to those tools except the owner's own aggregated channel statistics, which are used to plan future videos.
  • The App does not use cookies or tracking technologies, and this website does not either.

Retention and deletion

Analytics reports and tokens are kept only on the owner's computer, only while the owner keeps the App authorized, and are deleted when access is revoked or the App is removed. To revoke the App's access at any time, visit the Google security settings page and remove "YouTube Autopilot". Revoking access stops the App immediately. You can also ask us to delete stored data by emailing the address below, and we will delete it within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
