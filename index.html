Nicosia Studio Autopilot

A private publishing tool for three YouTube channels run by JT Nicosia.

What it is

Nicosia Studio Autopilot is a small program that runs on its owner's home computer. Each week it prepares new videos for the channels below, then uses the YouTube Data API to upload them, add titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, and schedule them to publish. It also reads each channel's own YouTube Analytics so the owner can see which videos are working.

It is used only by its owner, to manage channels the owner runs. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

The channels

The Little Ages

Short, fact-checked true history stories told in a miniature diorama style.

Hazel and Bo

An original animated series about friendship and feelings for children ages 3 to 5. Every video is marked "made for kids" on YouTube.

Retirement Rulebook

Plain-English explainers about Medicare and Social Security rules. Educational only, not financial or legal advice.

How it uses Google and YouTube

The tool signs in with Google OAuth and asks for permission to upload and manage videos and captions, and to read analytics, for the owner's own channels only. It does not access any other user's account, and it does not collect personal information about viewers. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
